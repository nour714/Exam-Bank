# Exam Bank — Product Roadmap

This document outlines the business features and product roadmap for the Exam Bank platform, aligned with the 2026 Egyptian Baccalaureate system.

## 1. Grade Expansion (1st & 2nd Secondary)
Expanding the platform beyond 3rd Secondary to cover all high school grades.
- **Grade & Pathway Selection**: First login selection (1st, 2nd, or 3rd Secondary). Pathways for 2nd/3rd (Medicine & Life Sciences, Engineering & Computer Science, Business, Arts & Humanities).
- **Dynamic Subject Filtering**: Dashboard filters subjects relevant to the student's grade and pathway.
- **Question Bank Schema Update**: Every question tracks `grade` and `pathway`.
- **Exam Attempt History**: Multi-attempt storage for official exams.

## 2. Core Learning Features (Phase 2)
- **Exam Mode**: Dedicated exam interface (no hints, countdown timer, flag for review).
- **Post-Exam Review**: Detailed review showing wrong answers and explanations.
- **Advanced Question Filtering**: Filter by exam year, source, and difficulty.

## 3. Retention & Engagement Features (Phase 3)
- **Daily Challenges**: 5 quick questions daily for XP and streaks.
- **Spaced Repetition System**: Incorrect questions re-queued at increasing intervals (Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14).
- **Leaderboards**: Weekly/monthly rankings by grade.
- **Badge System**: Virtual badges for achievements (e.g., 100 Physics questions, 7-day streak).

## 4. AI-Powered Features (Phase 4)
- **Weakness Detection**: AI analyzes wrong answers and generates custom quizzes targeting weak points.
- **AI Mock Exams**: 1-click generation of full exams matching official formats.

## 5. Social & Productivity Features (Phase 5)
- **Study Groups**: Competitive shared exams where members see each other's scores.
- **Pomodoro Timer**: Built-in focus timer (25/5) with ambient sounds.
